Vehicle-Vitals keeps every vehicle's service records, receipts, reminders, and running costs in one place — so you always know what's been done and what's due next.

Built for households and hands-on owners: keep one shared garage for every car, and hand a buyer a clean, complete service record when it's time to sell.

FEATURES
- Garage: track every vehicle you own, active or stored, with mileage and status at a glance
- Service records: log maintenance with date, mileage, cost, shop, notes, and photo attachments like receipts or invoices
- Maintenance plan: see upcoming and overdue work, snooze or dismiss reminders, and get notified before something is due
- Service history: a full chronological timeline of completed work for each vehicle
- VIN lookup: add a vehicle by its VIN to auto-fill make, model, and year
- Recall check: see open safety recalls reported for your vehicle
- Shops & services: find and save nearby providers
- Reminders: set lead time and driving assumptions, with calendar sync (Google, Apple, or ICS)
- Cross-platform: your garage stays in sync between the web app and iPhone

PLANS
Vehicle-Vitals is free to start. Pro and Premium plans raise the vehicle limit and add features; see current pricing in the app.

Vehicle-Vitals is built for anyone who wants a clear, organized record of vehicle ownership — private owners, DIY mechanics, and households managing more than one car.
